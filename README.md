Neon Harbor — a top-down open-world crime game set in the fictional coastal city of Port Meridian. Jax Marlowe owes the Harbor Kings $12,000, and fixer Delia Voss hands out ten story jobs to pay it down: a timed van delivery, stealing the red Comet S sports car, a pickup run at 3 wanted stars, a roadblock run with Moss to the harbor, a shootout with the Kings' armed enforcers at the east warehouse, a night of taxi fares, a cache run for five stashes, an informant escort through two ambushes, a five-gate street race against the clock, and a final armored-van score at 4 stars that clears the debt. Explore the city on foot, by car, and on the Falcon motorcycle — the fastest ride in Port Meridian — steal vehicles, outrun police cars and foot cops through a 0–5 star wanted system (cruisers now cut you off with predictive pursuit and park roadblocks across your escape at 4–5 stars), fight gunmen who shoot back with pistol and fists, grab cash, ammo and health pickups, hunt 8 hidden smuggler's caches, and watch the city fall dark as the day/night cycle turns the neon on. Hard driving leaves tyre marks on the road and gunfire flashes at the muzzle. A live minimap in the corner tracks the player, the mission marker, police cruisers, unfound caches, the Cache Run's stashes, the Street King gates, Kings enforcers and the informant — plus Delia's garage and the hospital. A procedural WebAudio soundtrack — bass groove, kick, hats and lead — plays under the engine, gunshots and sirens. Plain JavaScript canvas, static files, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD moves and drives, mouse aims, click shoots, F punches, E enters a car, R reloads, M mutes, P pauses. Find all 8 caches for a $500 bonus.
